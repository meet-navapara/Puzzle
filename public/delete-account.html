PetalGrid · Account deletion

Delete your account & data

Use this page for Google Play’s account-deletion requirement (com.petalgrid.game).

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PetalGrid and sign in with Google (if you use cloud saves).
  2. Go to Settings or Privacy & Legal.
  3. Tap Delete account & cloud data.
  4. Confirm Google Sign-In when asked (re-authentication), then confirm deletion.

This removes your Firebase Authentication user, deletes your Firestore document at users/{uid}, and clears local saves on that device.

Guest / local-only players

If you never signed in, use Clear local data on this device in Privacy & Legal / Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes on-device data.

Request by email

Email navaparameetdev@gmail.com from the Google account you used in the app.

Include:

  • Subject: PetalGrid account deletion
  • The Google email used to sign in
  • Optional: approximate last play date

We will delete associated Authentication and cloud-save data within a reasonable period (typically within 30 days) after verifying ownership.

What is deleted

  • Firebase Auth account for PetalGrid
  • Cloud Firestore save under your user ID
  • Local game progress on the device that runs in-app deletion

Advertising identifiers are managed by Google / your device settings and are not stored as a PetalGrid account profile.
