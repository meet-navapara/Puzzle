Support

Help for PetalGrid players on Android and iOS.

Common topics

  • Guest vs Google: Guest progress stays on this device. Sign in with Google to sync cloud saves.
  • Missing progress: Confirm you are signed into the same Google account used previously.
  • Ads: Development / test builds may show Google test ads. Production builds will use live AdMob units once configured. Never click your own ads.
  • Sound: Check in-app Settings and your device media volume.
  • Internet: An internet connection is currently required to enter play modes; local saves remain on device during brief disconnects.
  • Account / data deletion: See Delete account.
