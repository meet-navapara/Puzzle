PetalGrid · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · App: PetalGrid (com.petalgrid.game)

1. Overview

PetalGrid is a casual block-placement puzzle game. We collect only what is needed for gameplay saves, optional Google Sign-In, cloud sync, crash-free operation, and advertising SDKs when ads are enabled.

2. Information we collect

  • Local game data: progress, settings, scores, and guest identifiers stored on your device.
  • Account data (optional): if you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your account UID and basic profile identifiers provided by Google (such as email) for sign-in and cloud save linking.
  • Cloud saves: when signed in, progress may sync to Cloud Firestore under your user ID.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect device advertising identifiers and related data under Google’s policies. Test ad units may be used during development; production builds will use the publisher’s AdMob units when configured.
  • Connectivity check: the app may briefly contact a Google reachability endpoint to verify internet before entering play modes.
  • Firebase service data: information required to operate Firebase Authentication and Firestore (no separate Crashlytics/Analytics SDK is bundled at this time).

We do not request contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, or SMS permissions for core gameplay.

3. How we use information

  • Provide gameplay, saves, and optional cloud sync
  • Authenticate Google Sign-In users
  • Show ads (when enabled) and measure ad delivery
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse

4. Sharing

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) and Google Mobile Ads. Those processors handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention & deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage, use in-app clear/delete controls, or uninstall. Signed-in players can delete their account and cloud save in Settings / Privacy & Legal, or follow Delete account & data. You may also email navaparameetdev@gmail.com.

6. Children

PetalGrid is intended for a general audience of casual puzzle players, not directed at children under 13. The Play listing should not be declared as designed for children / Families unless child-directed ads and consent requirements are fully implemented.

7. Your choices

  • Play as a guest without Google Sign-In
  • Sign out or delete cloud-linked account data from in-app privacy controls
  • Control device ad personalization in your OS / Google settings
  • Mute sound and reduce motion effects in Settings
